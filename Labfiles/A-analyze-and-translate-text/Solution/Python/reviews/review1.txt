Superb lodge and outstanding staff
Alpine Ski House Zermatt, Zermatt, Switzerland
3/2/2026
Spotless rooms, excellent service, and a fantastic location right below the Matterhorn and a two minute walk from the Gornergrat railway. We thoroughly enjoyed our stay. The wellness terrace is wonderfully peaceful and we ate at the lodge restaurant, which serves alpine cuisine with a Michelin Star. We had the tasting menu and it was fabulous. The suites are very well appointed, with a kitchenette, lounge, bedroom, and an enormous bathroom. Contact me at alex@contoso.com for more details.
