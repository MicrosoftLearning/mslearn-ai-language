Great location and helpful staff, but close to the lift queue
Alpine Ski House Whistler, Whistler, Canada
8/16/2026
We stayed here in August after reading reviews. We were very pleased with the location, just behind the Whistler Village stroll, a lively area with plenty of restaurants to choose from. The Blackcomb base was lovely to wander through, with very interesting timber architecture. Make sure you walk up to the Audain Art Museum and out to Lost Lake for a good view of the valley and the peaks. It is on a shuttle route and easy to get into the village. Rooms were clean with plenty of space, and staff were friendly and helpful. The only downside was the noise from the gondola queue, so ask for a room furthest away from the lift base.
