Very noisy and the rooms are tiny
Alpine Ski House Whistler, Whistler, Canada
9/5/2026
The lodge sits directly beside the main gondola base, which runs from first lift until late at night, especially at weekends. The noise would not be so bad if the rooms were better insulated, but they are not. I had to put cotton wool in my ears to sleep and was too tired to enjoy the mountain the next day. The rooms are TINY. I picked our room because it had two queen size beds, but there was barely space to fit them. With a family of four in the room it was very tight. With all that said, the rooms are clean and they have made an effort to update them. The lodge is in Whistler Village with lots of good places to eat, within walking distance of Lost Lake. It may be a good lodge for young stay-up-late adults on a budget.
