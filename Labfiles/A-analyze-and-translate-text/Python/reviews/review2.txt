Tired lodge with poor service
Alpine Ski House Zermatt, Zermatt, Switzerland
5/6/2026
This is an old lodge (it has been here since the 1960s) and the room furnishings are average, becoming a bit dated now and in need of replacing. The internet did not work and I had to use one of their back office rooms to check in for my flight home. My colleague John Smith says it is the worst lodge he has ever stayed in, and I am inclined to agree!
